HVAC-R technician simulator · Coming Soon · Early Access pre-alpha, in development · Windows

Run your own HVAC-R service business. Take real-world style service calls, read gauges and meters, diagnose faults in homes and shops, make the repair, and grow your company. Easy mode trainer tips teach you the trade as you play.

Features

Features and content are subject to change. The game is in development.

  • Realistic service calls: no-cool, no-heat, short cycling, noisy units and more
  • Diagnose with refrigerant gauges, multimeter, thermometer and clamp meter
  • Repair or replace capacitors, contactors, motors, thermostats and other parts
  • Explore multi-room houses with kitchens, bathrooms, basements and back yards
  • Manage your shop, truck stock and money
  • Easy mode trainer tips explain each reading and step, great for students and new techs

Early Access FAQ

Coming Soon. No release date has been announced. Everything below is planned and subject to change.

Why Early Access? We're a small indie studio. We want players, and real techs, helping shape the game early so it gets realistic in the ways that matter.

How long will it be in Early Access? There is no set timeline. It depends on development progress and player feedback, and is subject to change.

How is the full version planned to differ? These are planned, not guaranteed, and subject to change:

  • More fault types and equipment (heat pumps, furnaces, mini-splits, commercial refrigeration)
  • Career progression, licenses and hiring
  • Higher-detail models and sounds
  • More neighborhoods and job sites
  • Achievements and Steam Cloud saves

What is the current state of the Early Access version? Playable core loop: service calls, diagnosis, repairs, shop, money and Easy mode. Expect placeholder art, rough edges and bugs.

Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? Pricing has not been decided yet and will be announced on the Steam store page.

How are you planning on involving the Community? Steam discussions, an in-game feedback button and email (alphamaxstudios@gmail.com), patch notes and roadmap updates. We may run playtests before larger updates.

System requirements

Minimum

  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: Quad-core 3.0 GHz (Intel i5-8400 / Ryzen 5 2600)
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 1060 6 GB / AMD RX 580 8 GB
  • DirectX: DirectX 11
  • Storage: 6 GB

Recommended

  • OS: Windows 10/11 64-bit
  • Processor: 8-core (Intel i7-10700 / Ryzen 7 3700X)
  • Memory: 16 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA RTX 3060 Ti / AMD RX 6700 XT
  • DirectX: DirectX 12
  • Storage: 6 GB SSD

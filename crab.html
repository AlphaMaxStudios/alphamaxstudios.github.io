Commercial crab fishing sim · Coming Soon · Early Access pre-alpha, in development · Windows

A realistic commercial crab fishing sim. Start with a small boat and a string of pots, then set, soak, haul and sell your catch. Work Home Harbor and the grounds beyond, read the weather and tide, upgrade your gear, and try to land the season note.

Wishlist on Steam Send feedback

Features

Features and content are subject to change. The game is in development.

  • Set, soak, haul and sell: bait your pots, run the strings, haul the catch and sell at the dock
  • Four grounds: Home Harbor, Nearshore Flats, Reef Edge and Fog Bank, with permits for the farther water
  • Weather, tide and day/night that change the catch and what the boat can take
  • Sort the catch: keepers, jumbos and rock crab pay; shorts and females go back
  • Upgrade your operation: pots, bait, hauler, live well, engine, sounder, hull plate and permits
  • Ranks from Greenhorn to Highliner, a chart, first-person deck and helm, and a four-day season note

Early Access FAQ

Coming Soon. No release date has been announced. Everything below is planned and subject to change.

Why Early Access? We're a small indie studio. We want players, and people who work the water, helping shape the game early so it gets realistic in the ways that matter.

How long will it be in Early Access? There is no set timeline. It depends on development progress and player feedback, and is subject to change.

How is the full version planned to differ? These are planned, not guaranteed, and subject to change:

  • More grounds and longer seasons
  • Online co-op and crew
  • Better boat, harbor and ocean visuals
  • Hands-on pot strings, baiting and a gauge at the rail
  • Achievements and Steam Cloud saves

What is the current state of the Early Access version? Playable: the set, soak, haul and sell loop, four grounds, weather and tides, shop upgrades, ranks, a chart, first-person deck and helm, and a four-day season note. Visuals, co-op and extra content are still being worked on.

Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? Pricing has not been decided yet and will be announced on the Steam store page.

How are you planning on involving the Community? Steam discussions, an in-game feedback button and email (alphamaxstudios@gmail.com), patch notes and roadmap updates. We may run playtests before larger updates.

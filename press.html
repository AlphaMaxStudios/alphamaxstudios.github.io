AlphaMax HVAC-R

Run your own HVAC-R service business. Take real-world style service calls, read gauges and meters, diagnose faults in homes and shops, make the repair, and grow your company. Easy mode trainer tips teach you the trade as you play.

AlphaMax Hunting: Whitetail

A realistic whitetail deer hunting sim. Real ballistics built from load data, organ-based wound damage and a 2 km wild map. Scout, pick your rifle, shotgun or handgun, read the wind and make an ethical shot, or practice out to 1000 yards on the range.

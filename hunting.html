Realistic whitetail hunting sim · Coming Soon · Early Access pre-alpha, in development · Windows

A realistic whitetail deer hunting sim. Real ballistics built from load data, organ-based wound damage and a 2 km wild map. Scout, pick your rifle, shotgun or handgun, read the wind and make an ethical shot, or practice out to 1000 yards on the range.

Features

Features and content are subject to change. The game is in development.

  • Real ballistics: drop, drift and energy from real load data
  • Organ-based wound damage: heart, lungs, liver and more, with realistic blood trails and recovery
  • 2 km open map with woods, fields and creek bottoms
  • Rifles, shotguns and handguns, each with its own handling
  • Whitetail AI that reacts to scent, sound and sight
  • 1000-yard shooting range to zero and practice

Early Access FAQ

Coming Soon. No release date has been announced. Everything below is planned and subject to change.

Why Early Access? We're a small indie studio. We want players, and real hunters, helping shape the game early so it gets realistic in the ways that matter.

How long will it be in Early Access? There is no set timeline. It depends on development progress and player feedback, and is subject to change.

How is the full version planned to differ? These are planned, not guaranteed, and subject to change:

  • Tree stands, ground blinds, calls, scents and feeders
  • Game warden, tags and seasons
  • Map, GPS and rangefinder
  • First-person arms, animations and better handling
  • More maps, more firearms and archery
  • Achievements and Steam Cloud saves

What is the current state of the Early Access version? Playable: the 2 km map, deer AI, ballistics, wound system, five firearms and the range. Visuals, animations and performance are still being worked on.

Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access? Pricing has not been decided yet and will be announced on the Steam store page.

How are you planning on involving the Community? Steam discussions, an in-game feedback button and email (alphamaxstudios@gmail.com), patch notes and roadmap updates. We may run playtests before larger updates.

System requirements

Minimum

  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: 6-core (Intel i5-10400 / Ryzen 5 3600)
  • Memory: 16 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 1660 Super / AMD RX 5600 XT
  • DirectX: DirectX 12
  • Storage: 20 GB SSD

Recommended

  • OS: Windows 10/11 64-bit
  • Processor: 8-core (Intel i7-12700 / Ryzen 7 7700X)
  • Memory: 32 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA RTX 3070 / AMD RX 6800
  • DirectX: DirectX 12
  • Storage: 20 GB SSD
